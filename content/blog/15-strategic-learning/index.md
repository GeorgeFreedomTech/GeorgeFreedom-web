## The Pace & The Problem
The sheer velocity of AI development is unprecedented. Gone are the days of comfortably mastering one tool before the next arrived. Now, it’s a constant flood of new models, libraries, and agents demanding attention. 

The core challenge isn’t just learning; it’s **strategic resource allocation** - our limited time and cognitive capacity - against a potentially infinite stream of novelties, many destined for rapid obsolescence. Chasing everything leads to burnout and shallow knowledge.

 
## The Strategy: Principles & Ruthless Prioritization
Survival and success in this environment demand ruthless prioritization and a **focus on enduring value**. Double down on fundamental principles (problem decomposition, system design, algorithmic thinking) that transcend specific tools. 

When evaluating new tech, ask critical questions: 
* What’s the real problem it solves for me? 
* What’s the genuine efficiency gain vs. the learning curve and potential short lifespan? 
* Does it align with my strategic goals, or is it just noise?

—

*We need to consciously **balance depth** in core areas with **broad awareness**, and choose our learning ‘battles’ strategically to invest energy where it yields lasting results, not just chase fleeting hype.*
